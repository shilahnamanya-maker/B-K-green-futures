About us

Organisation background

A community initiative that grew into a government-funded NGO working for a cleaner Kampala.

Our story

Started by the community, for the community

B&K Green Futures was founded as a community initiative in 2003 and was later funded by the Government of Uganda in 2011.

It started with the aim of collecting disposables, but we soon discovered that disposables can be recycled and made useful again. Today we connect young people and households to environmental action and the skills to earn from it.

Our journey

How we got here

  1. 2003

    A community initiative begins

    Neighbours come together to collect disposables and keep the area clean.

  2. 2011

    Government of Uganda funding

    Support from government lets the initiative grow its reach and programmes.

  3. Today

    Recycling, skills and eco-clubs

    Disposables are recycled and made useful again, and young people learn green skills.

Mission
